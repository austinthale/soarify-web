Google Business Profile Optimization & Management

Dominate local search and the Google map pack with hyper-optimized GBP strategies.

The Foundation of Local Visibility

Your Google Business Profile is the single most important asset for local search visibility. When potential customers search for your service in your area, the map pack appears first — and the top 3 positions capture 70% of all clicks.

What's Included

  • GBP Setup & Optimization: Complete profile audit, category and subcategory optimization, keyword-rich business descriptions, location-based service areas, and professional photo uploads with proper EXIF data.
  • Monthly Management: Weekly Google Posts, Q&A monitoring and response, fresh photo uploads, citation cleanup and consistency across the web, review response strategy, and local link building.
  • Performance Tracking: Monthly reporting on profile views, calls driven, direction requests, search clicks, and ranking movement across key local search terms.
  • NAP Consistency: We audit and fix your Name, Address, and Phone across hundreds of business directories — a critical ranking signal Google rewards.

Why It Matters

85% of local searches happen on Google. If your business profile is incomplete, poorly optimized, or buried below competitors, you're leaving revenue on the table. We rebuild your profile from the ground up to capture high-intent customers actively searching for your service in your area.

Expected Outcomes

Most clients see measurable map pack ranking improvements within 30 days, with calls and direction requests increasing by 40-120% over the first 90 days. Rankings compound — meaning the longer we work together, the more stable and dominant your position becomes.

Reputation Management & Review Strategy

Build trust through strategic review generation, monitoring, and response — without the awkward asking.

Your Reputation is Your Currency

75% of consumers trust reviews as much as personal recommendations. A business with 50+ five-star reviews on Google ranks higher, converts more customers, and charges premium prices. We build a systematic review-generation machine that brings in authentic customer feedback without being pushy.

What's Included

  • Review Generation Strategy: SMS and QR code-based review requests timed to peak customer satisfaction (right after delivery or service completion).
  • Review Monitoring: Daily alerts on new reviews across Google, Yelp, Facebook, and industry-specific platforms.
  • Response Management: Professional, personalized responses to every review — positive or negative — to build trust and show prospective customers you care.
  • Negative Review Handling: Calm, solution-focused responses that defuse tension and often convert upset customers into loyal advocates.
  • Reputation Reporting: Monthly tracking of review volume, average rating, sentiment trends, and impact on rankings and conversion.

Why It Matters

Google's ranking algorithm weighs review quantity, recency, and sentiment heavily in local search. A business with growing five-star reviews ranks higher than competitors with outdated or negative feedback. Reviews also give prospective customers confidence — increasing conversion rates by 30-50%.

Expected Outcomes

Average rating increase to 4.7+ stars, 30-50% more reviews per month, improved ranking visibility, and 20-30% higher conversion rates from existing traffic due to increased trust signals.

Google Local Services Ads (LSA) Setup & Management

Appear at the very top of Google search with trusted, pay-per-lead Local Services Ads.

The Premium Local Search Real Estate

Google Local Services Ads (LSA) appear above the map pack and standard paid ads — the most premium real estate on local search. These ads show a green "Google Guaranteed" badge and only charge you per qualified lead, not per click.

What's Included

  • LSA Account Setup: Eligibility assessment, service category selection, business verification, and background check coordination.
  • Profile Optimization: Service descriptions, coverage areas, pricing strategy, photo uploads, and FAQ setup to maximize lead quality.
  • Lead Quality Management: Response time optimization, lead screening, customer messaging integration, and feedback tracking.
  • Campaign Monitoring: Real-time tracking of impressions, leads, response rates, and cost-per-lead. Monthly optimization based on performance data.
  • Integration: We connect LSA leads to your CRM, booking system, or email so nothing falls through the cracks.

Why It Matters

LSA typically costs 30-60% less per lead than Google Ads, carries the "Google Guaranteed" trust badge (which boosts conversion), and appears at the absolute top of search — before map pack, before organic results. For service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, etc.), LSA often delivers the highest-quality, fastest-closing leads available.

Expected Outcomes

Average cost-per-lead 30-50% lower than paid ads, response times under 1 minute, and close rates 20-40% higher due to the "Google Guaranteed" badge and lead pre-qualification by Google.
