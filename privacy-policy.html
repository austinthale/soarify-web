Privacy Policy

Plain language, no legalese. Here's what this site collects, why, and how to reach us if you have questions.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy applies to soarify.net and all of its pages, including our blog (The Updraft) and the mobile "tap" page linked from our business cards.

Who we are

Soarify Digital Marketing, LLC ("Soarify," "we," "us") provides Google Business Profile optimization, local SEO, and related marketing services for local businesses. For privacy questions, contact us at info@soarify.net or +1(559)346-7123. We're based in Utah, USA.

What information we collect

Information you give us

When you request a free audit or contact us, you may provide:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your business name, website, and Google Business Profile link
  • Anything you write in the message or form fields

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we collect basic technical data when you visit:

  • IP address, browser type, and device type
  • Pages visited, time on site, and the page or link that referred you (for example, a tap on our NFC business card)
  • General location, such as city or region

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare your free Google Business Profile audit
  • To schedule calls, follow up, and send information about our services if you've asked for it
  • To understand how the site is used, fix problems, improve content, and keep it secure
What we don't do. We don't sell your personal information. We don't share it for third-party advertising. We don't add you to a marketing email list unless you clearly ask us to.

Third-party services we use

A few outside services help run this site. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Forms collects audit and contact requests. Responses are stored in our Google account.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) measures traffic and page performance. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on our pages, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • SearchAtlas provides on-page SEO optimization tooling that runs on the site.
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the site and keeps standard server logs (privacy policy).

Cookies

We use very few cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visitors apart and measure sessions, and our host may set basic session cookies. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and still use the whole site.

How long we keep your information

  • Form submissions: kept as long as they're relevant to our working relationship. Ask us anytime and we'll delete them.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics retains it for 14 months by default.
  • Server logs: kept by our host on its standard schedule.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Email info@soarify.net and we'll respond within 5 business days.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the sale of your information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, use the email above.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, tell us and we'll delete it.

Links to other sites

We link to other websites, including client sites, cited sources, and social media. We aren't responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top, and we'll note any material changes clearly. Continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Questions?
